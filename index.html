Roads

Abuja Interstate Expressway

FCT, Abuja

The Abuja Interstate Expressway is a major infrastructure initiative connecting key commercial hubs. The project involved laying extensive dual-carriageway systems, sophisticated drainage networks, and modern traffic management infrastructure. Completed ahead of schedule, it stands as a testament to Kelly B Ventures' capability in delivering large-scale road projects.
Buildings

Victoria Island Corporate HQ

Lagos State

A state-of-the-art commercial headquarters located in the heart of Victoria Island. The building features smart energy management systems, premium architectural finishes, and sustainable structural design. We successfully delivered this project following stringent international safety standards, creating an iconic landmark in the Lagos skyline.
Infrastructure

Delta Region Bridge Project

Delta State

This vital infrastructure project connects previously isolated communities across the Delta region. Utilizing advanced marine engineering techniques, the bridge spans over a key waterway. Our team successfully managed complex environmental challenges, delivering a durable structure designed for heavy-duty commercial transport and improved regional logistics.
