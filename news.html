Press Release

Kelly B Awarded Major Highway Expansion Contract

We are proud to announce that our firm has been awarded the contract to expand a vital trade corridor, enhancing interstate connectivity and economic growth...

Benin City, Nigeria — Kelly B Multipurpose Ventures is pleased to announce the official awarding of a major road infrastructure expansion project. The contract encompasses dualization, asphalt overlay, drainage construction, and bridge reinforcement along key economic corridors.

Speaking on the landmark contract, Managing Director Engr. Kelly B noted: "This award reflects our unwavering commitment to engineering precision, timely project delivery, and structural durability. Our team is fully prepared to deploy cutting-edge engineering methods to deliver an interstate corridor that stands the test of time."

Construction activities are set to commence immediately, prioritizing site safety, minimal traffic disruption, and local employment opportunities for community technicians.

Company Update

Advanced Engineering Solutions & Infrastructure Expansion

To boost our operational capacity across multiple federal and state sites, Kelly B has deployed next-generation machinery and digital project tracking tools...

In line with our corporate commitment to delivering high-grade civil infrastructure, Kelly B Multipurpose Ventures has integrated advanced surveying technology, heavy excavation units, and real-time site monitoring software.

This initiative significantly optimizes operational throughput across road building, foundation earthworks, and structural engineering projects in Southern and Western Nigeria.

HSE & Safety

Celebrating 1 Million Safe Man-Hours Without Incident

A monumental safety milestone for our team at the Delta Regional project site, achieving 1,000,000 continuous hours of zero-incident operations...

Safety is not just a policy at Kelly B Multipurpose Ventures; it is our primary operating culture. We are thrilled to report that our site engineering and field operations teams have officially achieved 1 million safe man-hours without a single Lost Time Incident (LTI).

This milestone reflects daily safety toolboxes, continuous hazard assessments, mandatory personal protective equipment (PPE) compliance, and rigorous HSE audits across all working environments.

We applaud all project managers, safety officers, and site personnel for maintaining world-class safety standards day in and day out.

Community & CSR

Local Youth Empowerment & Skill Acquisition Program Launched

Kelly B launches a community engineering apprenticeship program designed to train local youth in vocational construction skills and site safety...

As part of our Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) mission, Kelly B Multipurpose Ventures has launched a community skills acquisition program in Edo State.

The program provides hands-on technical training in concrete testing, heavy equipment operations, surveying fundamentals, and site safety management for emerging indigenous technicians.

Through direct community involvement, Kelly B continues to foster sustainable economic growth and local capacity building across all host communities.
